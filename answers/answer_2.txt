Answer: The new tests are wrong. The intuition of the programmer must have been that a population consisting of a single genotype should be maximally inbred, and therefore have an inbreeding coefficient of one. However, mathematically the inbreeding coefficient is simply not defined in such a case, because then the denominator (the heterozygosity) is zero. Therefore, the test must be adapted, and instead check that the output should be "not a number" (NaN). Take home message: tests are here to help, but they too can be wrong!!
